CampusDesk Teams in-page bridge reference
========================================

The read-only Teams Chat Service message endpoint, backwardLink pagination,
MSAL access-token cache/encryption format, local conversations index schema,
and attachment property names were reviewed from:
https://github.com/gediz/teams-web-chat-exporter
Commit ca606d2a9edcf07403986f58588f62a6c50e8b56
src/content/api-client.ts, src/content/api-converter.ts,
src/content/teams-state.ts, src/content/share-resolver.ts,
src/entrypoints/content.ts, src/utils/teams-urls.ts.

CampusDesk uses its own smaller bridge, not the browser extension. It enforces
current-account matching, exact scope matching, strict resource host/path
checks, no redirects, GET-only data operations (plus the exact Teams authz
service-discovery authentication exchange), bounded data and explicit partial
results. No upstream scripts were executed and no extension is installed.
These are internal Teams endpoints, not a guaranteed public Microsoft API.
The local conversation index is not proof of exhaustive account discovery.

Bridge limits: each attachment is capped at 12 MiB before base64 encoding;
callers may request a smaller maxBytes. JSON service pages are capped at
2,000,000 bytes; message results at 2,500,000 serialized characters; each job
is limited to at most 40 pages, 2,000 records, and 60 seconds. Native callers
must impose their own per-run attachment budget and bounded message framing.
